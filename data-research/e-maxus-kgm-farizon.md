## Maxus

SAIC Motor's LCV brand (successor to LDV in the UK). Maxus badge in the UK since 2019 (Deliver 9 / eDeliver ranges from 2020). T90EV no longer listed (superseded by eTerron 9).

## KGM (SsangYong)

KG Mobility, South Korea. UK importer rebranded SsangYong → KGM in early 2024; existing SsangYong-badged models (Tivoli, Korando, Korando e-Motion, Rexton, Musso) continued on sale. Current UK range (kgm-motors.co.uk, July 2026): Tivoli, Torres Hybrid, Torres EVX, Actyon Hybrid, Rexton (+ Rexton Commercial), Musso, Musso Rhino, Musso EV. Korando dropped ~2025; Korando e-Motion dropped 2024.
