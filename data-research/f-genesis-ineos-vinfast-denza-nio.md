Researched July 2026 for CarData.Wiki. All figures UK-market unless noted. null/blank = unverified, do not fabricate.

## Genesis
**UK launch:** Summer 2021 (G80 + GV80 first, then G70/GV70/Shooting Brake). **Key change:** during 2024 Genesis UK dropped all ICE models (G70 saloon, G70 Shooting Brake, petrol/diesel G80, GV70 ICE, GV80) — remaining stock sold through, no new imports. From 2024/25 the UK range is EV-only: GV60, Electrified GV70, Electrified G80 (all facelifted for 2025 with bigger batteries). Sources: https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/genesis-to-become-electric-only-brand , https://www.autocar.co.uk/car-news/new-cars/genesis-drops-g70-and-gv80-it-shifts-focus-hybrids

## INEOS
**UK launch:** 2022 (order books/production; first UK customer deliveries late 2022 / early 2023). INEOS Automotive is UK-headquartered (London), Grenadier built in Hambach, France. Lineup: Grenadier Station Wagon (5-seat), Grenadier Utility Wagon (2-seat N1 commercial), Grenadier Quartermaster (double-cab pickup, from late 2023). **Fusilier:** smaller EV/range-extender 4x4 announced Feb 2024 — delayed indefinitely (now ~2028) due to weak EV demand; NOT on sale. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ineos_Grenadier , https://www.autocar.co.uk/car-news/new-cars/ineos-delays-fusilier-indefinitely-over-electric-vehicle-uncertainty

Shared powertrain (all models): BMW 3.0-litre inline-6 (petrol B58 / diesel B57), ZF 8-speed automatic, permanent 4WD with two-speed transfer case, top speed limited to 160 kph (99 mph), braked towing 3500 kg. Trims: base, Trialmaster, Fieldmaster.

## VinFast
**UK status: NEVER LAUNCHED — plans cancelled.** VinFast announced UK ambitions (2022, targeting 2024 entry with VF8), appointed a UK MD in late 2023, but postponed the UK launch in autumn 2024 and subsequently abandoned it entirely, closing VinFast UK. No VinFast model has been sold new through UK retail channels. Europe strategy pivoted to franchised dealers in France/Germany/Netherlands only.
Sources: https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/vinfast-abandons-uk-launch-plans , https://www.am-online.com/news/vietnam-s-vinfast-has-ditched-plan-to-sell-cars-in-uk , https://theinterface.uk/blog-posts/vinfast-uk-launch-postponed-until-further-notice
