## GWM (incl. Ora)

**UK presence:** GWM entered the UK late 2022 with the Ora Funky Cat EV (brand "GWM Ora", imported by International Motors). Funky Cat renamed **Ora 03** in late 2023/2024 with a restructured Pure/Pro/GT range and big price cuts. **Ora 03 withdrawn from UK sale in early-mid 2026** (only 26 registrations Q1 2026). Haval Jolion Pro Hybrid went on sale February 2025 (£23,995). Poer300 diesel pick-up went on sale mid-September 2025. GWM plans ~7 new Haval-led models for Europe from mid-2026.

**Model list:** Ora Funky Cat / Ora 03 (2022–2026), Haval Jolion Pro Hybrid (2025–), Poer300 (2025–).

**Explicitly NOT UK-sold (verified):** Haval H6 / H6 GT PHEV (tested/considered but never officially launched in UK as of July 2026 — Australian-market model only in RHD), Wey 03/05 (EU-only, never UK), Ora 07, Tank models.
