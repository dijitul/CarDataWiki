# JAECOO & OMODA — UK Market Lineups (researched July 2026)

Both brands belong to Chery Automobile (China) and are distributed in the UK by Omoda & Jaecoo UK.
- OMODA UK launch: 30 August 2024 (Omoda 5 + Omoda E5 on sale simultaneously).
- JAECOO UK launch: orders late 2024, first UK deliveries 31 January 2025 (Jaecoo 7 petrol + 7 SHS PHEV).

Naming note: UK cars are badged "JAECOO 7", "OMODA 5" etc. (no J/O prefix). Chinese/global names J7, J5, J8, O5 map to UK 7, 5/E5, 8, 5/E5.

## General caveats
- Kerbweights largely unpublished by UK sources at research time — left null throughout rather than estimated.
- "MPG" for PHEVs is the WLTP weighted-combined figure and is not comparable with ICE mpg.
- Trim-level equipment differences (wheel sizes etc.) can shift CO2/mpg slightly between trims of the same powertrain.
