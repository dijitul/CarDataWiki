## Cross-cutting notes / data gaps

1. **Battery convention:** BYD quotes Blade battery capacity where usable ≈ gross; ev-database "gross" figures (62, 46, 84, 93 kWh) are estimates. JSON uses BYD/ev-database usable figure in `batteryKwh` with note.
2. **CO2 = 0 and combinedMpg = null for all EVs.**
3. **Unverified/null:** Seal 6 & Sealion 5 & Atto 2 DM-i torque, weights, wheelbases; Atto 2 Comfort 0-62; Dolphin Boost torque; Seal U DC charging rate; Dolphin Surf Active weight.
4. **Conflicts flagged:** Dolphin 44.9 DC 60 vs 65 kW; Seal U Boost mpg 403.9 vs 313.9; Seal boot 400 vs 485 L (MY26); Sealion 7 top speed 215 vs 216 kph; Atto 2 DM-i Active EV range 31 vs 24 mi; Seal 6 0-60/0-62 discrepancy.
5. **Discontinued variants:** Dolphin Active & Boost 44.9 kWh (2024–2025 only); Atto 3/Dolphin/Seal MY changes treated as continuous variants.
6. **Not included (not on UK sale as of July 2026):** Sealion 8, Shark 6, Dolphin G.
